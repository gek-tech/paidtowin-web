What it is about

Many games sell advantages without saying so. Paid to Win turns that around: spending money is the whole game. Every euro becomes points, and points decide your place in the ranking.

  • Rankings for the world, your continent, your country and your friends, for the current season and for all time.
  • Ranks and medals from Iron to Croesus. Only the top 100 in the world are Tycoons, only the top 10 are Croesus. A season lasts one year and has four acts. Every act starts from zero for everyone and awards a quarter medal in the rank you reached; at the end of the year the four quarters form the season medal.
  • Country duels every month between countries of similar strength. So that no single player can buy a duel, at most 10,000 points per person count each month.
  • Voluntary spending limit per month. Lowering it applies at once, raising or removing it only after seven days.

Paid to Win contains no gambling: no loot boxes, no random rewards and no prizes with monetary value. You always see exactly what you buy.
