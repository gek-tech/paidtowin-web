2. In short

  • We only process what the game needs: your account, player name, country, points, ranks, medals and purchases.
  • Your player name, country, points, ranks and medals are visible to other signed-in players. That is the point of a leaderboard.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see payment details such as card numbers.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account at any time.

3. What we process and why

3.1 Account and sign-in

You sign in with Apple, Google or a sign-in link sent by email. We receive your email address and an account identifier. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address; we then only receive a relay address from Apple. For security, technical data such as your IP address and the time of sign-in are logged.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract for using the app) and, for security logs, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in preventing abuse).

3.2 Profile and leaderboards

When you create your profile we store your player name, your country, the time you confirmed you are at least 18, and when you joined. From this and your purchases we calculate points, positions, ranks, medals and country duels. Player name, country, points, ranks, medals and the month you joined are visible to other signed-in players. If you follow other players, we store that.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Purchases

You buy points through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process the payment under their own responsibility and privacy policies. We only receive which package you bought and when, a transaction number and, where applicable, a refund. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases (see section 4). If Apple or Google refunds a purchase, we remove the points and the medals earned with them.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for keeping purchase records, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (commercial and tax law obligations).

3.4 Reports

If you report an offensive player name, we store who reported whom, when, why, and the reported name. The reported player is not told who reported them.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (obligations under the Digital Services Act) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a respectful game).

3.5 On your device

The app stores your sign-in on your device so you stay signed in, and your light or dark mode setting. This data does not leave your device, apart from the sign-in itself. Legal basis: Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.6 Contact by email

If you email us, we process your address and message to answer your request. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

3.7 Website

When you visit paidtowin.de, our host GitHub (see section 4) processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and fend off attacks. The website does not use cookies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Service providers

We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA): database, sign-in and sign-in emails. Data is stored on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc., USA): purchase verification. RevenueCat receives your account identifier, purchase data and technical information about your device and store.
  • GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA): website hosting.

For sign-in with Apple or Google, Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) and Google (Google Ireland Ltd., Ireland) are responsible for their own sign-in services.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Supabase, RevenueCat and GitHub are based in the USA, so data may be transferred there. Such transfers rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, and otherwise on the Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, points, medals and follows: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: as long as commercial and tax law requires (up to 10 years), then deleted.
  • Reports: until handled, at most 6 Monate afterwards.
  • Sign-in security logs: usually a few days to weeks.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings or ask us by email. We then delete your account, profile, points, medals and follows. Purchase records we are required to keep remain until the retention period ends, but are no longer linked to a player name.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Email us at support@paidtowin.de.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in your place of residence or the authority responsible for us: the Data Protection Commissioner of Lower Saxony (Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany, www.lfd.niedersachsen.de.

9. Minimum age

Paid to Win is for people aged 18 and over only. We do not knowingly process data of minors. If we learn of it, we delete the account.

10. No automated decisions

Leaderboards and medals are calculated automatically according to fixed rules. There is no automated decision with legal effect within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

11. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
